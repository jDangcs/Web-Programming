About Klyros Workspace




Picture of Our Founders

An intelligent execution platform designed for people who started on five planning apps a year and then end back up on sticky notes. Klyros workplace is here to bring your responsibilities, deadlines, projects, and time together all in one place.

I created Klyros Workspace because managing work, school, and personal responsibilities can become overwhelming and stress inducing very quickly when everything is spread out across multiple different apps, websites, calendars and notes or reminders. Assignments being posted on one website, work will come through via email or through texting, and personal responsibilities such as budgetting and internship as well as studying, all have their own separate place. I wanted to create one place where I and many other people could know what and when they need to do something, as well as see the progress they have made towards that task.

The idea of this application occured when I realized that staying organized is more than just simply making a list of tasks. A list can tell you what needs to be done, but it doesn't always help you decide which one to work on first or how to fit everything into the the time you have available. Klyros Workspace combines task management, project tracking, scheduling, and a built-in calendar so that users can turn their responsibilities into an organized plan. With an AI assistant and a planner, it can also help users prioritze their work and create realistic schedules based on their deadlines and available time.

Klyros Workspace is designed to be flexible enough for all different types of users. A student could use it to manage classes, assignments, exams, study sessions and even track their internship application and budgetting. Employees could use it to organzie their projects, meetings, and deadlines as well as using it to budget. The goal is to create one system that helps people understand what needs their attention, plan their time effectively, and keep moving toward their goals without having to manage everything across several different platforms.
