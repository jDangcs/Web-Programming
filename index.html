Klyros Workspace Features

Everything you need to plan your work, school, and life in one place.

Features

  • Task Management
  • Project Tracking
  • Scheduling & Calendar
  • AI Assistant
  • Budgeting

Task Management

Staying organized is more than just making a list of tasks. A list can tell you what needs to be done, but it doesn't always help you decide which one to work on first or how to fit everything into the time you have available. Klyros Workspace helps you turn a flat list into an actual plan.

Project Tracking

Whether it's a class project, a work assignment, or a personal goal, Klyros Workspace lets you track progress over time instead of just checking a box when something is "done." You can see how far along each project is at a glance.

Scheduling & Calendar

A built-in calendar ties your tasks, projects, and deadlines together in one view, so you're not juggling a separate app, website, and calendar just to see what's due when.

AI Assistant

The AI assistant helps prioritize your work and builds realistic schedules based on your deadlines and how much time you actually have available, instead of leaving you to figure that out manually every day.

Budgeting

Klyros Workspace isn't just for tasks and time. Students and businesses alike can use the built-in budgeting tools to keep their finances organized right alongside everything else they're managing.
